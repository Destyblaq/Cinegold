The Story Behind CineGold

CineGold was created with a simple idea: entertainment should be easy to discover, beautiful to experience and available whenever you want it.

We are building a platform where viewers can discover movies and series, create personal watchlists and enjoy a premium digital entertainment experience.

Our goal is to combine great content with a simple, modern and responsive platform that works across phones, tablets, laptops and desktop devices.
